# The World of Emily Hale

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/ChestnutHillHouse.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Girlhood Home**

The home in Chestnut Hill where Emily Hale lived from the age of five to twenty-six.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/FirstChurchChestnutHill.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Chestnut Hill Church<br>** First Church in Chestnut Hill, whiere Hale's father served as pastor from 1896 to 1918.

Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/blob.png/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**41 Brimmer Street<br>** This Boston home may have belonged to Mary Lee Ware, with whom Hale sometimes lived in the early 1930s. Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/blob-0009.png/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Brattle Hall**

Brattle Hall, home of the Cambridge Social Dramatic Club, where Hale and Eliot performed in 1913. Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/blob-0007.png/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Eleanor Hinkley's Home**

Eliot fell in love with Hale in 1913, rehearsing for a skit performed in this house near Harvard.

Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/1024px-UWM-0904-downer-quad.jpg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Milwaukee-Downer College<br>** The campus of what was then Milwaukee-Downer College, where Hale taught from 1921-29. <br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald <br>

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/PabstTheater-3-0003.jfif/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Pabst Theatre, Milwaukee (2021)<br>** Hale took the lead in the Wisconsin Players' production of "Miss Lulu Bett" here in 1922.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/TollHall.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Toll Hall, Scripps College<br>** Hale served as dorm matron here from 1932-1934.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/ClaremontTrainStation.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Claremont Train Station<br>** Hale met Eliot's train here when it arrived near dawn in late December 1932.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald<br>

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/BalboaIslandView-0001.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Balboa Island**

View across Newport Harbor to Balboa Island, where Hale and Eliot were taken to get time alone together.

Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/blob-0003.png/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Corona del Mar Beach** <br>
Hale and Eliot spent time together on this beach in early 1933.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald<br>
![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/BalchHall-0001.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Balch Hall**

Eliot delivered a public lecture here during his visit to Scripps College.

Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/PaduaHillsTheater-0001.jpeg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Padua Hills Theatre, Claremont** <br>
Hale performed here in 1933-34, while teaching at Scripps College.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/StamfordHouse.jpg/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Stamford House, Chipping Campden**

Eliot visited Hale at this Cotswold home from 1934-1939.

Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/cf3b878d-146c-4bd2-864f-413d644dca05/blob-16b3462.png/:/rs=w:1200,m)

**Andover Inn, Massachusetts<br>** Hale and Eliot dined at the inn on the Phillips Andover campus in spring 1956.<br>
Photo credit: Sara Fitzgerald
