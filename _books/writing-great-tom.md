# the story behind the story

**When T. S. Matthews set out to write the first biography of T. S. Eliot, he was so frustrated that he wrote a separate manuscript about his struggles. Here's that fascinating story, which I discovered in his papers at Princeton University Library. I wrote the Foreword, explaining the background.**
